Abstract— This document gives formatting instructions for authors preparing extended abstracts in Microsoft Word for publication in the programme and proceedings for the Off-school Advanced Learning Programme.  The authors are asked to follow the instructions given in the document when preparing their learning summary. You can use this document as both an instruction set and as a template into which you can type or paste your own text.

Introduction

[P12 section=2 loc=12 indent=@v5 tabs=[{"val":"left","leader":"none","pos":"378"}]]
This document is a template based on the IEEE template used for IEEE transactions.  This should make it relatively easy for extended abstracts submitted to be modified to form either a final IEEE proceedings paper.  If you have questions, please contact the TA or instructors.

[P13 section=2 loc=13 indent=@v5]
Finished papers should be submitted as Word or PDF files to the Google form which is announced through email.

[P14 section=2 loc=14 indent=@v2 spacing=@v3 numbering=@v4]
Page Layout

[P15 section=2 loc=15 indent=@v5]
An easy way to comply with the learning summary formatting requirements is to use this document as a template and simply type your text into it or use the paste function.

[P16 section=2 loc=16 indent=@v2 spacing=@v6 numbering={"num_id":"4","level":"0"}]
Page Layout

[P17 section=2 loc=17 indent=@v5]
The paper can be four pages maximum in length.  Your paper must use a page size corresponding to the US letter paper size, which is 216mm (8.5") wide and 279mm (11") long.  The margins must be set as follows:

[P18 section=2 loc=18 indent=@v7 numbering=@v8]
Top = 18mm (0.7")

[P19 section=2 loc=19 indent=@v7 numbering=@v8]
Bottom = 18mm (0.7")

[P20 section=2 loc=20 indent=@v7 numbering=@v8]
Left = Right = 16.5mm (0.65")

[P21 section=2 loc=21 indent=@v5]
Your paper must be in one-column format.  Do not edit the header or footer of the document.  Page numbers will be inserted later.

[P22 section=2 loc=22 indent=@v2 spacing=@v3 numbering=@v4]
Page Style

[P23 section=2 loc=23 indent=@v5]
All paragraphs must be indented.  All paragraphs must be justified, i.e. both left-justified and right-justified.

[P24 section=2 loc=24 indent=@v2 spacing=@v6 numbering=@v9]
Text Font of Entire Document

[P25 section=2 loc=25 indent=@v5]
The entire document should be in Times New Roman or Times font.  Other font types may be used if needed for special purposes.  Recommended font sizes are shown in Table 1.

[P26 section=2 loc=26 indent=@v2 spacing=@v6 numbering=@v9]

[P27 section=2 loc=27 indent=@v5]
Title must be in 24 pt Regular font.  Author name must be in 11 pt Regular font.  Author affiliation must be in 10 pt Italic.  Contact email address must be in 9 pt Courier Regular font.

[P61 section=2 loc=31 indent=@v5]
All title and author details must be in single-column format and must be centered.

[P62 section=2 loc=32 indent=@v5]
To avoid confusion, the family name must be written as the last part of each author name (e.g. John A.K. Smith).

[P63 section=2 loc=33 indent=@v5]
Each affiliation must include, at the very least, the name of the institute/university and the name of the country where the author is based (e.g. University of Tokyo, Japan).

[P64 section=2 loc=34 indent=@v5]
Email address is compulsory for the corresponding author.

[P65 section=2 loc=35 indent=@v2 spacing=@v6 numbering=@v9]
Figures and Tables

[P66 section=2 loc=36 indent=@v5]
Figures and tables must be centered in the column.  Large figures and tables may span across both columns.  Any table or figure that takes up more than 1 column width must be positioned either at the top or at the bottom of the page.

[P67 section=2 loc=37 indent=@v5]
Graphics may be full colour.  All colours will be retained in the online proceedings but will be gray scale in the printed proceedings.  Graphics must not use stipple fill patterns because they may not be reproduced properly.  Please use only SOLID FILL colours which contrast well both on screen and on a black-and-white hardcopy, as shown in Fig. 1.

[P78 section=2 loc=48 indent=@v2 spacing=@v6 numbering=@v9]
Figure Captions

[P79 section=2 loc=49 indent=@v5]
Figures must be numbered using Arabic numerals.  Figure captions must be in 8 pt Regular font.  Captions of a single-line (e.g. Fig. 2) must be centered whereas multi-line captions must be justified (e.g. Fig. 1).  Captions with figure numbers must be placed after their associated figures, as shown in Fig. 1.

Table Captions

[P86 section=2 loc=56 indent=@v5]
Tables must be numbered using uppercase Roman numerals.  Table captions must be centred and in 8 pt Regular font with small caps.  Every word in a table caption must be capitalised except for short minor words as listed in Section III-B.  Captions with table numbers must be placed before their associated tables, as shown in Table 1.

Links and Bookmarks

[P92 section=2 loc=62 indent=@v5]
All hypertext links and section bookmarks will be removed from papers during the processing of papers for publication.  If you need to refer to an Internet email address or URL in your paper, you must type out the address or URL fully in Regular font.

[P93 section=2 loc=63 indent=@v2 spacing=@v6 numbering=@v9]
References

[P94 section=2 loc=64 indent=@v5]
The heading of the References section must not be numbered.  All reference items must be in 8 pt font.  Please use Regular and Italic styles to distinguish different fields as shown in the References section.  Number the reference items consecutively in square brackets (e.g. [1]).  

[P95 section=2 loc=65 indent=@v5]
When referring to a reference item, please simply use the reference number, as in [2].  Do not use “Ref. [3]” or “Reference [3]” except at the beginning of a sentence, e.g. “Reference [3] shows …”.  Multiple references are each numbered with separate brackets (e.g. [2], [3], [4]–[6]).
